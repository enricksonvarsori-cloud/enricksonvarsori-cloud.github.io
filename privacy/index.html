Privacy notice

Site responsibility

varsori.com is the personal academic website of Enrickson Varsori. It provides information about research, projects, publications, impact and professional contact details.

For privacy-related questions, you can contact varsori@dct.uc.pt.

Analytics and technical information

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate website usage and performance. According to Cloudflare, this analytics service does not collect or use visitors' personal data, does not use cookies or localStorage for analytics, and does not fingerprint individuals for analytics purposes.

The service may provide aggregate information such as page views, referrers, countries and website performance metrics. It is used to understand how the site performs and which pages are being visited, rather than to identify individual visitors.

Read about Cloudflare Web Analytics

Direct contact

The site does not currently provide user accounts, comments, newsletter subscriptions or web forms that collect personal information directly.

If you contact Enrickson Varsori by email, the information you choose to include in that message will be processed only as necessary to read and respond to your enquiry and to maintain relevant professional correspondence.

External websites and services

This website links to external services including ORCID, Google Scholar, ResearchGate, LinkedIn, CECS and academic publishers. When you follow an external link, that service operates under its own privacy and data-protection practices.

varsori.com does not control how those third-party websites process information after you leave this site.

Your privacy rights

Where personal data is processed in connection with direct correspondence, applicable data-protection rights may include access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, depending on the circumstances and legal basis involved.

Questions or requests can be sent to varsori@dct.uc.pt.

Changes to this notice

This privacy notice may be updated if the site's functionality, analytics tools or data practices change. The date shown in the footer indicates the current published version.
