Research in practice

Beyond the page.

My work often sits between research, technology, institutions and the people expected to use, shape or respond to scientific knowledge.

Impact is not a single endpoint. It emerges through participation, evaluation, translation and interdisciplinarity.

In practice

Where these pathways take shape.

Participation Engage

Engaging people in research

Research becomes stronger when the people expected to use, shape or respond to it are part of the process.

I contribute to stakeholder engagement, co-creation, workshops and early-adopter activities that connect research teams with communities, practitioners and institutional actors.

Stakeholder engagement · Co-creation · Workshops · Early adopters

Evaluation Evaluate

Evaluating technology in context

Usability matters most when technologies meet real users, tasks and contexts.

My work combines usability, user experience and contextual evaluation across digital platforms, educational games, interfaces and participatory technologies.

Usability · User experience · Interfaces · Contextual evaluation

Translation Translate

Translating research into use

Research outputs gain value when they travel beyond academic publication and inform practice.

I contribute to science communication, exploitation, training and uptake activities that help research reach decision-makers, professionals and wider publics.

Science communication · Exploitation · Training · Research uptake

Interdisciplinarity Connect

Connecting disciplines and institutions

Complex problems often require research to move across disciplinary and institutional boundaries.

My work connects communication, sustainability, HCI, education and emerging technologies across European and international research environments.

Communication · Sustainability · HCI · Emerging technologies
