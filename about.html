About

Academic biography & research ecosystem

Enrickson Varsori is a researcher in Communication Sciences whose work explores how people interact with technologies, institutions and contemporary social and environmental challenges. His research spans digital media, hyperconnectivity and temporal experience, human–computer interaction, sustainability, science communication and emerging technologies, combining quantitative, qualitative and participatory approaches.

He currently works at the University of Coimbra within the Faculty of Sciences and Technology and is an Integrated Researcher at the Communication and Society Research Centre (CECS), University of Minho. His trajectory has been developed across European and international research projects and academic networks, including work in Portugal, Brazil and broader international collaborations.
