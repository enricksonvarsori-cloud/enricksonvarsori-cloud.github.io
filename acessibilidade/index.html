Accessibility

Accessibility statement

This website is designed and maintained with accessibility and usability in mind. The aim is to follow recognised good practices so that content remains clear, operable and usable across different devices, browsers and access needs.

Approach

Accessibility is treated as an ongoing design and maintenance practice rather than as a one-off certification exercise. The site is reviewed against relevant principles from the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG), with particular attention to perceivability, operability, understandable interaction and robust HTML structure.

Good-practice accessibility review in place

This statement does not claim formal certification or an accessibility seal. It documents the practical measures used to improve access to the site.

Good practices implemented

The site uses semantic headings and landmarks, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus states, skip links, responsive layouts, descriptive link text, sufficient touch targets, controlled carousel behaviour and colour combinations reviewed for contrast.

Images and decorative elements are handled so that meaningful content remains available to assistive technologies, while purely decorative visuals are excluded where appropriate.

Testing and review

The main pages have been reviewed with automated accessibility checks and manual inspection. This includes keyboard navigation, focus order, semantic structure, responsive behaviour, contrast, motion controls and consistency of interactive components.

Automated tools are useful for detecting technical issues, but they do not establish complete accessibility on their own. Manual review remains part of the site's maintenance process.

View the AccessMonitor assessment for the homepage

Known limitations

No website can guarantee an identical experience across every assistive technology, browser or device. Some third-party destinations linked from this site are outside the control of varsori.com and may follow different accessibility practices.

If an accessibility barrier is identified, it will be treated as a maintenance issue and reviewed where technically feasible.

Feedback and contact
