Research landscape

Four interconnected areas.

Distinct lines of inquiry, connected by a shared interest in how people, technologies and institutions shape one another.

01

Digital life & temporalities

How does continuous connectivity reshape attention, time and everyday social experience?

My work examines hyperconnectivity, subjective online time and digital practices, particularly how young people experience increasingly continuous technological environments.

Hyperconnectivity Subjective time Digital media Youth
02

HCI & usability

How should digital systems be evaluated when use is situated, social and context-dependent?

I study usability, user experience and interaction with digital platforms and interfaces, with particular attention to evaluation in real-world contexts.

Usability User experience Interfaces Evaluation
03

Sustainability & participation

How can scientific knowledge become usable, participatory and meaningful to the people expected to act on it?

This work brings together environmental communication, ocean literacy, stakeholder engagement and public participation, focusing on how research is understood, used and taken up in practice.

Environmental communication Stakeholders Participation Ocean literacy
04

AI & emerging technologies

How do emerging technologies reshape communication, judgement and everyday practices?

I examine the human, ethical and communicative dimensions of artificial intelligence and generative technologies, especially where they intersect with education, time and social experience.

Generative AI Ethics Communication Emerging technology

Across these areas

Across these areas, the same questions recur.

Three recurring questions connect otherwise distinct research contexts, from digital life and usability to sustainability and emerging technologies.

01 Experience

How do people live with technology?

Looking beyond access and adoption to attention, perception, routines, emotion and social meaning.

02 Evaluation

How do we know whether a technology works for its users?

Bringing usability, user experience and contextual evaluation together rather than relying on isolated metrics.

03 Translation

How does research move into practice?

Examining how communication, stakeholder engagement and institutional conditions shape research uptake.
