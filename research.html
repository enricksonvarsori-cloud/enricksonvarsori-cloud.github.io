Research

Research across communication, technology and society

My research examines how people use, experience and evaluate technologies, and how communication shapes responses to social, environmental and technological change.

Communication & Digital Media

Research on digital communication and the everyday uses and practices of hyperconnected technologies, with particular attention to young people, temporal experience, attention and digitally mediated social behaviour.

Human–Computer Interaction

Research on usability, user experience and the evaluation of digital platforms, interfaces and technology-mediated interactions.

Sustainability & Environmental Communication

Research on communication, engagement and behavioural dimensions of climate, ocean and environmental challenges, including participatory and stakeholder-oriented approaches.

Artificial Intelligence & Ethics

Research on the ethical, normative and communicative dimensions of artificial intelligence and generative technologies.
